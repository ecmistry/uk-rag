# Free-only high-street tracker

Constraint: **no paid products** (no Beauclair, CACI, BRC member monitors, CRA QMA full report).

## Honest limit

Free sources **cannot** give:

- Catchment spend or leakage in pounds, every quarter, for named towns
- A single UK-wide shop vacancy rate by region every quarter (the BRC–LDC regional table is membership)

They **can** give: monthly GB regional footfall, quarterly NI property vacancy, GB headline vacancy when agents publish a free PDF, and **town-level vacancy/mix** whenever a council publishes a health check.

## Quarterly pack (repeatable)

| # | Download | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ONS UK retail footfall (xlsx) | https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/economicoutputandproductivity/output/datasets/ukretailfootfall |
| 2 | ONS real-time indicators (latest bulletin) | https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/economicoutputandproductivity/output/bulletins/economicactivityandsocialchangeintheukrealtimeindicators/latest |
| 3 | LPS NI quarterly vacancy CSVs | https://admin.opendatani.gov.uk/en_GB/dataset/quarterly-property-vacancy-rates-by-district-council-and-sector |
| 4 | CRA public charity-shop stats | https://charityretail.org.uk/charity-shop-stats |
| 5 | Search new free agent notes | `Savills OR "Knight Frank" OR "Green Street" high street vacancy 2026 pdf` |
| 6 | Contrast-town PDFs | search `[place] town centre health check vacancy` |

ONS footfall: aggregate the **three months** of the quarter; report YoY not MoM. NI is suppressed in that dataset.

## Contrast towns — free PDFs already in the briefing

- Bradford / Leeds — council AMR, BBC, planning evidence
- Sheffield — council retail study; Meadowhall named in local reporting
- Newport / Cardiff — council monitoring; Welsh Government town-centre papers
- Reading — Commercial Needs Assessment health checks (free PDF)
- Hanley — Stoke-on-Trent Retail and Leisure Study Vol. 3
- County Durham — 2025 council table (Peterlee, Bishop Auckland, etc.)
- Belfast and other NI districts — LPS (the only free **quarterly** vacancy geography)

## Mix (vape / barber / charity) without a paid survey

- **Charity:** CRA public totals; local Goad/health-check “charity shop” counts where the council publishes them (e.g. Cambridge, Derry occupancy study)
- **Barbers:** council use-class tables (E / retail services / hair); ONS SIC 96.02 business counts (annual, not high-street polygons)
- **Vape:** Scotland NVP register (free, outlets not specialist shops); England — planning/FOI and Trading Standards press; ASH surveys (GB, not town)

Do not merge these into one “filler index”.

## Property companies (free layer only)

Use **published research notes**, not their subscription platforms:

- Savills / Knight Frank retail updates (GB vacancy headlines, often citing Green Street/LDC)
- PwC annual openings/closures press release (regional net chain change)
- Centre for Cities *Checking out* — one-off July 2025, modelled vacancy for many cities — **argument only**
